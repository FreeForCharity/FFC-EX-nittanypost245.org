## Email Body

**Bottom Line Up Front:** The June 18, 2026 Post 245 meeting elected the 2027 officers, welcomed 2 new members, and recapped a successful BBQ that raised $2,123.56. The parking lot will be sealcoated July 12–13 (club closed both days). Final 2026 membership stands at 659 — 20 short of our 100% goal.

---

Comrades,

Here is a quick recap of the June 18, 2026 stated meeting. Full minutes are available online and as a PDF download below.

**Key Decisions & Highlights:**

- 💰 **Finance:** Post balance is **$22,324.18** as of June 18 (after $9,692.23 in checks and $16,258.84 in deposits). Savings account holds $10,112.17.
- 📊 **Membership:** Final 2026 count is **659 members — 20 short of our 100% goal.** Class A: 659 • Social: 147.
- 🗳️ **2027 Officers Elected:** Commander D. J. Watkins, 1st Vice Dennis Clark, 2nd Vice Rich Jackson, Treasurer Chris Solo, Adjutant Mitch DeLong. With one nominee per office, the Adjutant cast one vote.
- 🍖 **BBQ a success:** raised **$2,123.56**; the Silver Eagle rifle raffle raised $1,160.00 and private donations totaled $3,000.00.
- 🅿️ **Parking lot sealcoating July 12–13, 2026** — the club will be closed both days.
- 👤 **2 New Members accepted** (Brandon J. Sosa, Theresa L. Maher) — passed 14-0.
- 🎆 **July 4th Parade is set** with about 35 entries — volunteers needed (including a truck/driver for the Honor Guard and parade marshals).
- 🍺 **Oktoberfest planning underway** — volunteer availability will determine its size.
- ⚖️ **Skill Machines ruling:** The Supreme Court ruled them illegal with a 120-day stay; the Post is waiting for clarity before determining a way forward.
- 🎖️ **Honor Guard** performed 4 services for Memorial Day weekend and stood for one funeral last month.
